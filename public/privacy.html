Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

🔒 Privacy in Plain English
GuitarHelp is a free hobby project run by the owner of the GitHub repository onemorepereira/guitar-collection. This page explains what we collect, who can see it, and how long it's kept. It describes how the app works today.

1. The Short Version

  • What we collect: your email, name, and the guitars, photos and documents you add.
  • Who can see your guitars: only you, unless you create a share link or put a guitar in the public gallery (both are off until you turn them on).
  • AI: uploaded documents are read automatically by AI services on Amazon Web Services (AWS); some features you choose also use AI. See section 4.
  • Your control: export everything or delete your account yourself, on the Account page.
  • What we don't do: sell your data, use cookies, run analytics or ad trackers, or send marketing email.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Server logs (kept 90 days) record technical details of requests and errors.
  • For security, they record your IP address and browser user agent when some requests are refused as unauthorized and when you delete a guitar or revoke a share link. A guitar deletion also logs its brand, model and year and how many photos, documents and shares it had. Our application logs don't record email addresses.
  • When AI processing runs, file and document names may be logged; when an AI response can't be read, excerpts of the response and of the error (which can contain text from your document) are logged.
  • Share-link views: each view of a share link is recorded on that share (time, browser family, and the referring page, which is normally this site; no IP address) and shown to the share owner, so they can see how often the link is opened. The newest 1,000 views are kept until the share is revoked, its guitar is deleted, or the account is deleted. Each view also writes a server log line with the share and the referring page (no IP address).
  • Sign-in security history: Amazon Cognito keeps a security history of sign-in attempts. Sign-in goes through our server without device or browser data, so Cognito doesn't receive your IP address or device details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the app: store and show your collection, sign you in, reset your password.
  • To read your documents and power the AI features (section 4).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems, using the logs above.
  • To comply with the law if required.

We never sell your data, and we never use your photos in marketing.

4. AI Processing

Some features send your content to AI services on AWS:

  • Automatically, when you upload a document: PDFs are read by Amazon Textract, and the text is cleaned up by the Amazon Nova model on Amazon Bedrock. Images are read by Amazon Bedrock, which extracts their text and writes a description.
  • When you ask: receipt extraction (the receipt file or text you give it goes to Amazon Bedrock); specs import (the text or file you give it goes to Amazon Bedrock); provenance and sales reports (the guitar's details, private info such as purchase price, place and serial number, notes, condition-report notes, your name (provenance reports only), and for each linked document its name, notes, AI description and up to 2,000 characters of its extracted text go to Amazon Bedrock).

How AWS treats that content:

  • Amazon Textract: AWS may store and use content processed by Amazon Textract to improve the service; we haven't opted out.
  • Amazon Bedrock: under the default setting, AWS may retain prompts and responses for abuse detection.

5. Who Can See Your Data

By default, only you can see your guitars. Others see them only in these ways:

  • Share links: you create a link for a guitar and pick which fields it shows. Anyone with the link sees those fields and the photos. Revoking the link makes the page stop working.
  • Public gallery: off by default and turned on per guitar. It shows that guitar's brand, model, year, type, color, finish, materials, pickups, scale length, frets, strings, country of origin and photos on the public landing page, never notes, private info or the serial number. Turning it off takes effect within about 5 minutes.
  • Service providers: the companies that run the service for us (section 11).
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or to protect rights and safety.
⚠️ About photo links:
  • Photos and files (including receipts and documents) are stored at public, unguessable addresses. A photo link a visitor already received from a share page or the gallery may keep working after you revoke the share or leave the gallery; it stops working when the photo is deleted (or your account is). Share links use their own copies of your photos, which are deleted when you revoke the share, remove the photo or delete the guitar. Browsers may keep showing a cached photo for a while (see Data Retention).
  • Gallery photo links, and share photo links when a share has no copies of its own, contain a random account ID, so a visitor can tell that photos belong to the same person (but not who it is).
  • Photos up to 2048 pixels are stored as uploaded; larger ones are resized in your browser first. An original photo can carry metadata, such as camera details and the location if your phone recorded it, and that original is what the gallery and some share pages show. Share copies are re-encoded without it.

6. Your Rights & Control

  • Edit or delete any guitar, photo or document in the app. Deleting a guitar also deletes its share links and their view history; removing a photo removes it from your share links.
  • Export your data: on the Account page, with your password, you get a ZIP of all your data and files, usually ready within minutes and downloadable for 7 days.
  • Delete your account: on the Account page, with your password. Deletion completes in about 20 minutes; then the retention table applies.
  • Questions or requests: see Contact Us.

7. Cookies & Browser Storage

We don't use cookies, analytics, or ad or social trackers.

Your browser stores, for this site:

  • sign-in tokens and your cached profile (removed when you sign out);
  • recent autocomplete entries (kept after sign-out; removed when you delete your account on that device or clear the site's data);
  • theme and view preferences (kept until you clear the site's data).

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google's servers receive your IP address and browser details. This page and the Terms page don't load them.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not intended for children under 13. If we find an account belonging to a child under 13, we delete it. Parents can reach us as described in Contact Us.

10. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Public files and the app's pages may be cached at CDN locations in North America and Europe.

11. Third-Party Services

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosting, storage and the CDN; Amazon Cognito for sign-in and password-reset emails; Amazon Textract and Amazon Bedrock for AI processing (section 4).
  • Google Fonts: the app's fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details.

We don't use any analytics service.

12. How We Protect Your Data

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Database tables are encrypted at rest with AWS KMS keys; stored files are encrypted at rest by Amazon S3.
  • Database point-in-time backups are kept for 35 days.
⚠️ Important: No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your own copies of important information; the export on the Account page makes that easy.

13. Changes to This Policy
